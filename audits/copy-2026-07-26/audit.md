# Bulakao Gateway Copy Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-07-26

**Surface:** `http://localhost:4175/`

**User goal:** Understand what Bulakao does, see credible value in each practice, and continue to the relevant site.

## Overall verdict

The page is structurally clear, but the copy still sounds like polished service-category language. It names the two practices without giving either one a memorable mechanism, a concrete before-and-after, or much reason to believe Bulakao will run the work differently.

## Step 1 — Hero

**Health:** Needs a sharper parent promise.

Current:

> Bulakao coordinates creator campaigns and builds software for growing teams.

Strengths:

- Immediately names both areas of work.
- Avoids the earlier path and momentum metaphors.
- Supporting copy explains which practice owns each service.

Risks:

- The headline is a long description of the company structure, not a strong customer-facing value proposition.
- “Growing teams” is broad and widely used.
- “Coordinates” and “builds” describe activities but not the improvement customers receive.
- On mobile, the long sentence becomes a large block of eight lines.
- The body mostly repeats the headline at a lower level.

Improvement:

- Give the parent brand one shared customer problem: loose handoffs, unclear ownership, or manual coordination.
- Let the body assign the two practices without making the headline explain the business architecture.

Recommended direction:

> **Campaigns and operations, with fewer loose ends.**
>
> Partner coordinates creator campaigns from matching to reporting. Solutions replaces manual workflows with internal tools, automation, and applied AI.

## Step 2 — Partner

**Health:** Clear category; generic promise.

Current:

> Creator campaigns with the right fit and clear execution.

Strengths:

- “Creator campaigns” is concrete.
- The section covers matching, briefs, coordination, and reporting.
- Both brands and creators are represented.

Risks:

- “Right fit” is undefined: audience fit, brand fit, campaign fit, or values fit.
- “Clear execution” sounds like agency table stakes.
- The body uses “help” twice and spreads attention across brands, creators, and coordination without establishing ownership.
- The value rows are service labels rather than customer outcomes.
- “Explore Partner” does not indicate what the visitor will find next.

Improvement:

- Define fit in terms of audience and campaign.
- State that Bulakao owns the coordination from outreach through reporting.
- Replace generic nouns with specific work customers no longer need to chase.

Recommended direction:

> **Relevant creators. Clear terms. One owner from brief to report.**
>
> Partner helps brands find creators who fit the audience and campaign, helps creators assess the opportunity, and keeps the work moving through delivery and reporting.

Suggested value rows:

1. Matching based on audience and campaign fit
2. Briefs, deliverables, timelines, and approvals
3. Coordination from outreach through reporting

Suggested CTA:

> **See how Partner works**

## Step 3 — Solutions

**Health:** Understandable offer; familiar agency language.

Current:

> Software built around the work your team actually does.

Strengths:

- The section is anchored in real operational work.
- “One manual workflow at a time” introduces a useful scope constraint.
- The capability list is easy to scan.

Risks:

- “Built around the way your team works” is common agency copy; “actually” does not make it more specific.
- “Focused internal tools, automation, and applied AI” lists capabilities without showing the operational change.
- “Practical AI” is a category claim, not evidence of value.
- The section does not mention the strong part of the Solutions approach: starting from an existing spreadsheet, handoff, approval, or report and improving from actual use.
- “Explore Solutions” is passive.

Improvement:

- Lead with the first concrete change: replacing one manual workflow.
- Name the raw material customers already have.
- Frame iteration from real use as the differentiator.

Recommended direction:

> **Replace one manual workflow with software your team can use.**
>
> Solutions starts with the spreadsheet, handoff, approval, or report causing the most friction, then builds the tool and improves it from real use.

Suggested value rows:

1. Start with one costly manual workflow
2. Build around existing tools and handoffs
3. Improve from real use, not a theoretical spec

Suggested CTA:

> **See what Solutions builds**

## Highest-impact changes

1. Replace the hero’s company-description sentence with one shared customer problem.
2. Make Partner’s promise specific to audience fit, terms, and ownership.
3. Make Solutions lead with one manual workflow rather than a list of technical capabilities.
4. Rewrite value rows as concrete work or outcomes.
5. Replace both “Explore” CTAs with language that previews the destination.
