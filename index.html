FAQ

Before you send the rough version.

Start rough. These answers explain what to send, what happens next, and what kinds of tools Bulakao can build.

What should I send?

Send the spreadsheet, chat thread, screenshots, form, report, or plain-English description of the work your team keeps patching by hand.

What happens after I send it?

Bulakao reviews the rough version, identifies the first useful thing to build, and replies with a practical next step. If it works, we keep improving the system with you.

Do I need a polished brief?

No. Rough is better than theoretical. The current messy version shows the real handoffs, edge cases, and decisions the software needs to support.

What can Bulakao build?

Approval queues, operations dashboards, client portals, inventory trackers, booking systems, internal admin tools, and reporting systems.

Can you work with our current tools?

Usually, yes. The goal is to reduce manual drag around the tools you already use, not force a full rebuild on day one.

How small can we start?

Small is preferred. A focused first tool should handle real work and prove value quickly. From there, it can grow into a larger system through monthly improvements.
